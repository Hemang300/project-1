If you're in it right now

Short, practical, no lectures.

In a cab or auto
  • Photograph the number plate and send it to someone.
  • Share the live trip from the app.
  • Sit in the back, door unlocked on your side.
  • If the route feels wrong, get out at low speed — not on the highway.
Walking, late or alone
  • One earphone out, or none.
  • Walk facing traffic, away from parked vehicles.
  • Being followed? Cross the road. Go to a shop, pump, hospital or lobby.
  • Shout what's happening — "this man is following me".
  • A loud whistle or phone siren beats keys between fingers.
Harassment at work
  • Write it down the same day: date, time, words, witnesses.
  • Workplaces with 10+ staff must have an Internal Committee under POSH Act, 2013.
  • Fewer than 10? Local Committee via District Officer, or SHe-Box portal.
  • File within three months; extension possible.
  • Conciliation only if you want it — never a money settlement.
Online harassment, stalking, leaked photos
  • Screenshot with URL, handle, timestamp — before blocking.
  • Report at cybercrime.gov.in — anonymous option available.
  • Intimate images: file with platform under NCII policy too.
  • Money lost? Call 1930 immediately.
  • Sextortion is a crime against you. Don't pay.
Violence at home
  • DV Act 2005 covers physical, sexual, verbal, emotional, economic abuse.
  • Right to stay in the shared household.
  • Every district has a Protection Officer and One Stop Centre. Call 181.
  • Keep ID, papers, cash somewhere else.
  • Get injuries examined and keep the paper.

Your rights

What Indian law already gives you.

Any police station, anywhere

A Zero FIR can be filed at any station. "Not our area" is not a valid refusal.

BNSS, 2023

An FIR cannot be refused

For cognizable offences, registration is mandatory and free. You get a free copy.

Your statement, your terms

In sexual offence cases, recorded by a woman officer, at a place you choose.

Arrest timings

A woman should not ordinarily be arrested before 6am or after 6pm, only by a woman officer.

Free treatment, no FIR needed first

Every hospital must give free first aid to survivors, and inform police themselves.

No two-finger test

Banned by the Supreme Court. Any doctor conducting one is committing misconduct.

You can report late

No time limit on reporting a sexual offence.

Every workplace with 10+ staff needs a committee

Internal Committee with a senior woman and an external member.

POSH Act, 2013

Smaller workplaces are still covered

Local Committee via District Officer, or SHe-Box portal.

Relief while the inquiry runs

Transfer, up to 3 months paid leave, or stop him from reporting on your work.

Retaliation is not allowed

Being sidelined after complaining is actionable.

26 weeks of paid maternity leave

For first two children. Creche if 50+ employees.

Maternity Benefit Act, 2017

Equal pay for the same work

Sex-based pay discrimination is prohibited.

Code on Wages, 2019

Abuse is more than bruises

Physical, sexual, verbal, emotional, economic — all covered.

DV Act, 2005

It applies to live-in relationships

You don't have to be married.

The right to stay in the house

You can't be evicted from a shared household.

Orders a magistrate can pass

Protection, residence, monetary relief, custody, compensation.

Cruelty and dowry are crimes

Demanding, giving or taking dowry is punishable.

BNS s.85; Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961

Streedhan is yours alone

Husband and in-laws are only custodians.

Maintenance

A wife (or divorced wife not remarried) can claim maintenance.

BNSS s.144

Daughters inherit equally

A daughter is a coparcener from birth.

HSA 2005; Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma

Marriage doesn't end it

Marrying doesn't remove your share.

Banking on your own

No male signature needed for accounts, loans, property.

Your salary is yours

Being made to hand it over is economic abuse.

Lower stamp duty

Most states charge women a reduced rate.

Your name stays out of it

Publishing a survivor's identity is a crime.

Intimate images must come down in 24 hours

Includes morphed and AI-generated.

IT Rules, 2021

Report anonymously

cybercrime.gov.in has a section for crimes against women.

Online stalking is stalking

Repeated contact or monitoring is a crime.

BNS s.78

Sextortion is a crime against you

Don't pay. Screenshot, report, tell someone.

A lawyer costs you nothing

Every woman is entitled to free legal aid.

Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987

Trials held in private

Sexual offence trials in camera, before a woman judge where possible.

Your past is not on trial

Character or previous sexual history is inadmissible on consent.

Compensation, separate from verdict

Victim compensation schemes via Legal Services Authority.

You must be kept informed

About investigation progress; heard before bail.

India's criminal laws were replaced in July 2024 — IPC and CrPC became BNS and BNSS, but these protections carried over. This page is general information, not legal advice. For anything specific, call 15100.
